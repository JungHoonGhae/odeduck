# Code of Conduct

odeduck is a small open-source project, but the standard is simple: make it easier for another person to understand,
verify, and improve the work.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific, especially when disagreeing.
- Critique claims, code, and evidence rather than people.
- Assume mistakes are possible and make corrections easy.
- Protect private account, credential, and personal-data details.
- Credit sources and prior work accurately.

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, deliberate disclosure of private information, and repeated
disruption are not accepted in repository spaces or project-related communication.

Report conduct concerns privately to `lucas.ghae@remodule.dev`. The maintainer may edit or remove content, close or
lock threads, reject contributions, or restrict participation when needed to keep the project safe and constructive.
